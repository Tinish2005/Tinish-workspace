# DeepHire

## Problem

Traditional interviews frequently rely on subjective evaluation and provide limited measurable feedback. Candidates often do not receive detailed insights into communication quality, confidence, technical responses, or overall interview performance.

## What I Built

DeepHire is an AI-powered interview assessment platform designed to evaluate candidate interviews using multiple AI techniques.

The system supports interview question delivery, answer recording, candidate analysis, scoring, and feedback generation.

The platform follows a structured loop-based development process with validation gates and modular architecture.
